CASA House Calgary

A new two-storey youth mental health centre for CASA Mental Health on a 3.81-acre site in northeast Calgary's District at Stoney, bringing a live-in CASA House and an Adolescent Day Program under one roof. It's one of four centres in CASA's roughly $110M capital expansion, delivered by Clark Builders through Integrated Project Delivery.

I joined at the validation phase, long before work started on site, and stayed through construction, where I rolled out PCS, a lean project control system, across the integrated team.

About

Builder by trade, coder by curiosity.

I graduated from BCIT in 2004 with a building science background, then started in Vancouver on residential towers like One and Two Harbour Green, then moved through hospitality, aviation, retail and office interiors before settling in Calgary.

Today I'm a Senior Project Manager at Clark Builders, part of Turner, working on IPD and lean projects. I spend a lot of my time mentoring the people around me and those newer to the industry. I built this site myself while learning to code.
